# ⚡ EnerTwin AI

> **A Digital Brain for Energy Systems**

EnerTwin AI is an intelligent energy optimization platform that combines **AI/ML, Digital Twin, anomaly detection, forecasting, optimization, and Generative AI** to transform energy data into actionable decisions.

## 🚨 Problem

Traditional energy monitoring shows **what happened**, but not:

- Where energy is being wasted
- Why anomalies occur
- What will happen next
- What action can reduce cost and consumption

## 💡 Solution

EnerTwin AI provides a complete intelligence pipeline:

**Data → Diagnosis → Forecast → Simulation → Optimization → Decision**

### Key Features

- 🧬 **Digital Twin** — Visualize Campus → Building → Floor → Room → Equipment
- 🚨 **AI Anomaly Detection** — Identify abnormal energy consumption
- 🧠 **Root-Cause Analysis** — Explain why energy waste occurs
- 🔮 **Forecasting** — Predict future consumption and peak demand
- 🎛️ **What-If Simulation** — Test different energy-saving strategies
- 🏆 **AI Optimization** — Recommend the best intervention
- 💰 **Cost & ROI Analysis** — Estimate financial savings
- 🌱 **CO₂ Impact** — Measure environmental benefits
- 🤖 **GenAI Energy Analyst** — Get intelligent insights and automated reports

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

**Frontend:** Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Three.js  
**Backend:** Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL  
**AI/ML:** Scikit-learn, XGBoost, Pandas, NumPy  
**GenAI:** LLM, RAG, LangGraph  
**Deployment:** Docker, Vercel, Cloud
