HVAC efficiency
HVAC systems are a major campus energy load. Scheduling, setpoint resets, and occupancy-based control can reduce unnecessary cooling and heating demand.

Energy conservation
Energy conservation starts with reducing avoidable loads, aligning schedules to occupancy, and optimizing equipment runtime during off-peak hours.

Building energy management
Building energy management systems coordinate HVAC, lighting, and equipment loads to reduce peak demand and maintain comfortable conditions.

Peak demand management
Peak demand can be reduced by shifting non-critical equipment loads outside the daily peak window and trimming HVAC demand when occupancy is low.

Lighting efficiency
Occupancy sensors, daylight dimming, and LED retrofits reduce lighting loads while preserving comfort and safety.
